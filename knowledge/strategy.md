# Strategy and Walkthroughs

No combat build, farming route, quest walkthrough, or economy strategy has been validated yet.

The quickstart help recommends exploring for experience while checking risk, spending experience through level advances to reduce potential loss on death, then moving to combat experience when easy new rooms run out (suggesting at least level 10, perhaps 20). It points to a `stuff to kill` section in `help getting started`. This is generic help advice, not a personalized or tested route; inspect the character's build and the relevant help before acting on it.

Each future guide should state the character assumptions, prerequisites, steps, expected output, risks (death, item loss, cost, hostile rooms), recovery route, and date tested. Prefer repeatable observations over one-off success. Do not test attacks, purchases, trades, or item destruction as part of passive research.

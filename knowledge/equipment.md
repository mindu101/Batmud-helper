## Protection model (help text; not independently tested)

The live `help armours` entry says the protection shown by `eq` reduces damage taken. It describes protection as depending on racial skin protection, amount and type of armour, body slots covered, active protection spells, Combat Sense, and incoming attack type. Racial protection is added to other protection; armour protection is calculated from covered slots and item effectiveness rather than being directly added to the displayed protection value. Combat Sense improves the protection gained from armour and spells and is reflected in `eq`. The help recommends matching protection to likely attack types, and says damage messages can help identify those types.

This is documentation evidence only. No `eq` inspection or combat comparison has been performed for the current character. The source capture is [2026-10-22-armours.md](../sources/in-game-help/2026-10-22-armours.md); it has a clipped pager transition and incomplete final sentence.

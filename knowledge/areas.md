# Areas and Travel

## Known location

- **Dortlewall, Northern gatehouse** appeared in the live client during a help session. A north command may have been sent after a pager ended, so the current location is uncertain. The room description mentioned a tutorial portal and a locked north gate, with south as the obvious exit.
- The help index lists `exits`, `peer`, `look`, `blook`, and `map` as movement/information commands; `help movement` and `help city map` are promising references.

No route beyond that room has been verified in this research pass. Record exact room names, exits, travel commands, transport costs, hazards, and return routes as separate observations. Do not fill gaps by assuming compass directions imply adjacency.

The quickstart help points to granite posts outside areas and says `touch post` transports the character to a portal room with exits to several areas. It also locates adventurers' guilds in Dortlewall, Pleasantville, Arelium, and Calythien, and says Damogran is 1 east and 2 north of the church altar, with warehouse portals in Dortlewall and Pleasantville. These routes and transport behavior have not been visited or checked in game.

## Reincarnation overview

```text
Reincarnation is a way of starting over but keeping (most of) your achieved
experience points. During reincarnation you get to choose a new background,
race, alignment and all the things you chose when you created your character;
your name remains the same. You keep task points, quests, explore, age and
settings. After reincarnation, your character will be very young again.

For normal reincarnation or reanimation, you need to be at least level 20.
This limitation does not apply to newbies who can use 'recreate'.
The experience tax depends on the method and time since your previous reinc;
more time lowers the tax.
```

## Recreate

```text
Command: recreate
Reincarnates your character if it is under level 40 and less than 10d old.
This process is free both money and expwise. You can choose normal and
undead races with this method.
There is a 1h cooldown period between recreations.
```

## Process and taxes (partial)

```text
The reincarnation process has four methods and one flag; it is menu based.
Three non-instant methods start at 15000 experience after reincarnation and
return reincarnation experience at an accelerated rate while you earn exp.
The instant method returns experience immediately as safe experience but has
the highest tax. The menu displays exact tax and the experience needed to
recover reincarnation experience.

After reincarnation there is a 4-hour period when one re-reincarnation is
possible with 0.5% tax, but it remains locked to the prior method unless the
higher tax is accepted to change method.
```

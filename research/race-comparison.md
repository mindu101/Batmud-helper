# Merchant Race Comparison

Checked 2026-10-22 against public BatMUD race pages and historical player guides. Stat bars below are the values shown on the official race page, not a complete character build simulation.

| Race | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | Experience note |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Hobbit | 45 | 90 | 67 | 55 | 68 | Official page says Hobbits need less experience; slow HP/SP regeneration and short lifespan. |
| Sprite | 28 | 100 | 30 | 65 | 70 | Official page says Sprites need more experience; short lifespan. |
| Drow | 50 | 75 | 50 | 65 | 90 | Official page says Drow need more experience. |
| Orc (current) | 77 | 67 | 83 | 33 | 40 | Official page says Orcs need more experience; strong natural armour but slow HP/SP regeneration and short lifespan. |

Finaldey's 1996 merchant guide specifically names Hobbits and Sprites as suitable because of Wisdom/Intelligence and experience rate. That advice is old. For this level-17 merchant reset, Hobbit is the practical provisional candidate: solid WIS, reasonable INT/CON, high DEX, and lower experience requirements. Sprite trades survivability and experience efficiency for better INT/WIS; it is a higher-cost specialist choice. Drow offers the highest WIS among these candidates but has higher experience cost and is less efficient as a default new merchant. The user's stated preference about appearance/playstyle should decide among viable races.

Race stat bars and public page text can change; check the in-game race selection/help immediately before making a character change.
